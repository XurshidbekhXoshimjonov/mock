Great. Now, what research methods did you use?

I thought face-to-face interviews would be more appropriate, because you can look at things in more depth when you're actually with the person.
Hmm. How many participants did you have?
Quite a few, which meant interviewing was pretty demanding work. 25 altogether – 14 men and 11 women.
How did you identify them?
Well, they were all students at this college, and what I did was send round a really short questionnaire by email asking whether people liked maths,
and these 25 answered not at all, which is what I needed.

And I should emphasise that for this there were no distinct differences between men and women.
Did those figures surprise you?
I wouldn't say so. What did shock me was how violently anti-maths as a subject they were. It wasn't what I'd expected at all.
